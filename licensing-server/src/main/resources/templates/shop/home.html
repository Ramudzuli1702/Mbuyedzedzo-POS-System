About

Why "Mbuyedzedzo"

Mbuyedzedzo means restoration. It's the name for what good software should do for a small shop: give the owner back the hours a till receipt book, a stack of invoices and a manual stock count used to cost them every week.

That's really the whole idea: a point of sale that keeps your stock numbers honest, keeps a paper trail for every sale, exchange and return, and doesn't fall over the moment the internet does. The till app, the licence, and the phone scanner are all one system built by one team, not three different vendors stitched together.
