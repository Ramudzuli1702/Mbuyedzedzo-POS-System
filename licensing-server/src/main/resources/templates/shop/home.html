About

Why "Mbuyedzedzo"

Mbuyedzedzo means restoration. It's the name for what good software should do for a small shop: give the owner back the hours a till receipt book, a stack of invoices and a manual stock count used to cost them every week.

That's the whole brief behind this product — a point of sale that stays honest about stock, keeps a paper trail for every sale, exchange and return, and doesn't fall over the moment the internet does. It's built as one small, coherent system end to end: the till app on your counter, the licence that keeps it legitimately yours, and the phone in your pocket that can scan for it — rather than three different vendors stitched together.
